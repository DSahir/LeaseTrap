FICTIONAL SAMPLE LEASE. This is not a real agreement and not legal advice.

RESIDENTIAL LEASE
Town: Amherst, Massachusetts

1. Parties
This lease is between Maple and Mill Properties LLC, called the Landlord, and Jordan Hale, called the Tenant. Priya Shah will share the apartment as a roommate. Ellen Hale signs as guarantor. The manager is Casey Ortiz. Questions can be sent to jordan.hale.example@mailinator.com or 413.555.0148.

2. Premises
The apartment is 18 Kellogg Avenue, Apt 3, Amherst, MA 01002. It is a fictional unit used only to demonstrate this educational tool. No person named here is real.

3. Term
The term starts on September 1, 2026 and ends on August 31, 2027. Rent is $1,600 per month, due on the first day of each month.

4. Move-in payments
The security deposit is $2,400. Tenant shall pay a turnover stipend of $400 at move-in. Tenant shall pay a non-refundable professional cleaning fee of $250 before receiving keys. These amounts are written here so a reader can practice reviewing fees. They are not a bill.

5. Late fee
A late fee of $50 is due if rent is paid after the 5th day of the month. The fee is stated as a flat charge for this sample.

6. Deposit account
The security deposit shall be held in a separate interest-bearing account. The landlord will give the tenant the bank name and the account number that the landlord uses for that deposit. This sentence is the sample's ordinary, more protective clause.

7. Entry
Landlord may enter the apartment at any time without notice. The sample includes this sentence so a reader can see an entry rule that is worth a question.

8. Renewal
This lease renews automatically for 12 months unless Tenant gives 90 days written notice. Notice under this sample must be on paper and delivered to the manager.

9. Liability
All tenants are jointly and severally liable for the full rent and all other charges. Each person who signs may be asked for the whole amount. This is a question to raise, not a prediction of what a court would do.

10. Use of the apartment
The apartment is for housing only. Guests may stay up to seven nights in a row unless the manager agrees in writing to a longer stay. The tenant will not run a business from the unit. Quiet hours in this fictional building are 10 p.m. to 8 a.m.

11. Care of the unit
The tenant will keep the unit clean and will tell the manager promptly about leaks, heat loss, or a detector that chirps. The tenant is not describing a repair request by reading this sample. Ordinary wear is expected over a school year.

12. Utilities
The tenant pays electricity and internet. Heat and water stay in the landlord's name in this sample. The tenant will not let the indoor temperature fall low enough to freeze pipes.

13. Smoke and carbon monoxide detectors
The landlord provides detectors as required for a dwelling and tests them before move-in. The tenant will not remove a detector. A chirping detector should be reported to the manager the same day.

14. Subletting
The tenant will not sublet or assign the lease unless the landlord agrees in writing first. A request should name the proposed person and the dates. Silence is not agreement in this sample.

15. Rules
No smoking inside the apartment. Bicycles go in the rack by the side door, not in the hallway. Parking, if offered, is one space in the rear lot and is not part of the rent.

16. End of term
When the term ends, the tenant will return keys to Casey Ortiz. The tenant will leave the unit broom clean. The sample does not require a professional cleaner in this section; the cleaning fee above is a separate move-in charge.

17. Records used only for the scrubber demo
Date of birth for the fictional tenant: DOB: 03/14/2002. Sample tax identifier: SSN: 900-12-3456. Sample account note: account 123456789. These values are fake and exist so the preview can show masking.
