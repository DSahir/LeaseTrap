FICTIONAL CONTROL LEASE — no real people or property

The monthly rent is $1,500, payable on the first day of each month. The tenant may pay by check or electronic transfer.

The security deposit is one month's rent. The landlord will provide the required receipt and hold the deposit as required by Massachusetts law.

The landlord may enter only for a purpose allowed by law. The tenant should receive reasonable notice except when notice is not required.

The landlord will address repair requests as required by applicable housing standards. The tenant is responsible for electricity; the landlord pays for heat and water.

This fixed-term lease ends on August 31, 2027. Any extension must be agreed to in a writing signed by both parties.
